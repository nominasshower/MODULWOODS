      About us

      The beginning of Modul Woods.

      Modular Vanity Tops was founded in 2004, specializing in the manufacturing of high-quality cultured marble vanity tops. Over the years, we expanded our product portfolio to include additional vanity top styles and materials, continually evolving to meet the needs of our customers.

      In 2018, we launched Modul Woods, our custom cabinetry division, extending our expertise beyond countertops to include premium vanity and kitchen cabinets. Today, we manufacture frameless cabinetry for bathrooms and kitchens, offering vanity cabinets, base and wall cabinets, tall pantry units, islands, and custom storage solutions.

      Our products serve residential, hospitality, multifamily, senior living, and commercial projects, providing customers with a complete, coordinated solution from a single manufacturing partner. Whether supplying a single home or a large-scale hotel or multifamily development, we are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship, dependable quality, and innovative designs that bring every space to life.

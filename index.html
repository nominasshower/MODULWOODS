About us

The beginning of Modul Woods.

Modular Vanity Tops started in 2004 exclusively producing cultured marble vanity tops. After expanding into additional vanity top styles and materials, in 2018 Modul Woods launched as our premium vanity cabinet facility — created to perfect and complement our existing catalog.

Our vanity cabinets are built for a wide range of clients within the residential, hospitality and multi-family channels. The synergy between vanity tops and cabinets lets us adapt to the needs of every project — from single homes to hotels, motels and assisted-living communities.
